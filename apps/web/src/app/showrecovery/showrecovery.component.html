Account Backup and Recovery

The recovery word pattern below is needed to recover your account and decrypt your data if you lose access to all your Quick Crypt passkeys. If you lose both your passkeys and your recovery sheet, you will permanently lose access to your encrypted data. Because these words can only be displayed once, please follow these steps carefully:

  1. Print your emergency recovery sheet now, which includes your word pattern.
  2. Store the printout in a secure and durable location, separate from your passkeys.
  3. Optionally, also store just the recovery word pattern in a password manager.
  4. Do not allow untrusted parties to access any of your recovery information.

We recommend printing a physical copy of your emergency recovery sheet and storing the printout offline because someone with access to your recovery words and your user credential could delete your Quick Crypt account and weaken your encryption strength.

{{ sheetUserCred }}
