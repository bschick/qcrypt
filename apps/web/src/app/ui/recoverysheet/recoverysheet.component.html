User Credential

{{ userCred }}

Your user credential, along with your encryption passwords, is used to create the keys that encrypt and decrypt your data. Normally you do not need to know your user credential, but it is provided here for offline storage in case your Quick Crypt account is unintentionally deleted. If that ever happens, you can use the credential above with the Quick Crypt command-line tool to decrypt your data.
