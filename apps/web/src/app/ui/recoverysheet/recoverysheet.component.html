Quick Crypt Recovery Information

This information is needed to recover your account and decrypt your data if you lose access to your Quick Crypt passkeys. Print and store this sheet offline somewhere that is physically secure and durable, such as a lockbox. Do not allow others to access this recovery sheet.

Recovery Word Pattern:

{{ recoveryWords }}

This word pattern allows you to restore your account if you lose access to all your Quick Crypt passkeys. To restore your account, go to {{ host }}/recovery3 and enter the words in the exact order they appear above.

If someone you do not trust gains access to your recovery words but has not used them, go to {{ host }}/regenrecovery and change your recovery word pattern. If an untrusted party has used or changed your recovery words, your encrypted data is still protected by encryption passwords, but your account's overall security is permanently weakened. We recommend creating a new Quick Crypt account, re-encrypting your data, and then deleting your previous account.

User Credential:

{{ userCred }}

Your user credential, along with your encryption passwords, is used to create the keys that encrypt and decrypt your data. Normally you do not need to know your user credential, but it is provided here for offline storage in case your Quick Crypt account is unintentionally deleted. If that ever happens, you can use the credential above with the Quick Crypt command-line tool to decrypt your data.

Unlike the recovery word pattern, your user credential can never be changed. If this sheet is ever exposed to untrusted parties, your encrypted data is still protected by encryption passwords, but your account's overall security is permanently weakened. We recommend creating a new Quick Crypt account, re-encrypting your data, and then deleting your previous account.
